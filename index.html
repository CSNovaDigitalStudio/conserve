INDUSTRIAL SERVICES

SANDBLASTING

Industrial surface preparation and scale removal from metal and concrete surfaces. Specialized abrasive blasting for sensitive materials to ensure proper adhesion for protective coatings.

CLADDING

Supply and installation of industrial and architectural cladding systems (Aluminum, PVC, Metal). Solutions for insulation protection, weatherproofing, and aesthetic finishes.

INSULATION (HOT & COLD)

Thermal insulation to reduce heat transfer and energy consumption. Acoustic insulation to minimize noise and vibration. Installation for walls, ceilings, roofing systems, tanks, and vessels.

SPECIALIZED SERVICES

SPECIALIZED PAINTING & COATINGS

Surface preparation and application of protective industrial coatings (epoxy, anti-rust, heat-resistant). Corrosion control in accordance with project specifications. Interior and exterior painting for offices, factories, and warehouses.

CARPENTRY

Custom joinery including cabinets, shelving, and structural woodwork. Fittings, furniture, and storage solutions tailored to client needs.

UNDERGROUND PROTECTION

Installation, protection, and corrosion prevention of underground pipelines using approved Denso wrapping systems.
